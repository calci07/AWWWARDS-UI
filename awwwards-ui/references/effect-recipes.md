# Accessible, implementation-conscious effect recipes

Choose at most one signature recipe and one supporting family unless the approved route requires more. Implement the strongest static reading path first. Do not force a library: prefer repository-native capabilities and add dependencies only when justified.

## Recipe contract

For every effect define purpose, success signal, semantic/static state, trigger, interruption, responsive transformation, asset needs, layer ownership, performance risk, pointer/touch/keyboard behavior, reduced motion, and removal condition.

Prefer transform and opacity over layout-changing animation; bound continuous work; clean up listeners, observers, timers, and animation contexts; avoid forced synchronous layout; reserve media dimensions; pause offscreen work; and test low-power/coarse-pointer conditions.

## Matched material reveal

**Fit:** plain/treated, raw/refined, before/after. Stack two registered images derived from one master; lock canvas, camera, crop, silhouette, landmarks, light, and shadow. A clipped overlay may follow a bounded pointer or range control.

**Parity:** labeled toggle/slider for touch and keyboard; reduced motion uses direct crossfade or side-by-side. Reject drift, hidden evidence, or an effect-only control.

## Scroll chapter progression

**Fit:** sequence, transformation, case study, provenance. Use semantic ordered chapters and progressively enhance with intersection or scroll progress. Keep headings and progress legible.

**Parity:** mobile uses short vertical chapters or explicit steps; reduced motion removes scrub/pinning. Reject trapped scroll, excessive pinning, or unpredictable document height.

## Optical lens

**Fit:** texture, ingredient, craft, scientific or product detail. Magnify the same master in a bounded clipped layer; explicit hotspots provide authoritative details.

**Parity:** touch opens a detail sheet; keyboard traverses hotspots; static view includes a labeled detail crop. Reject decorative magnification or perpetual pointer tracking.

## Crop portals

**Fit:** editorial rhythm from one source. Reuse one image across two to four clipped regions with controlled object positions.

**Parity:** mobile keeps the strongest crop; static layout communicates without motion. Reject noise, duplicate downloads, misleading plurality, or excessive paint.

## Evidence stepper

**Fit:** real sequence such as baseline to insight to action, onboarding, or process. Use semantic ordered content with current-step state and direct controls.

**Parity:** previous/next plus direct steps; all content remains accessible without swipe. Reject arbitrary equal cards masquerading as a sequence.

## Variable-type response

**Fit:** a licensed variable font and concept make a real axis meaningful. Animate supported axes sparingly without disturbing reading order.

**Parity:** explicit control or content progression; reduced motion uses the most legible state. Reject reflow, unstable line boxes, or novelty-only use.

## Product frame sequence

**Fit:** verified assembly, rotation, transformation, or ritual. Use a consistent approved frame set, responsive poster, and deferred loading.

**Parity:** scrubber plus step controls; reduced motion uses poster and key details. Reject invented geometry, heavy eager downloads, or a fallback that loses essential meaning.

## Editorial collage

**Fit:** culture, archive, portfolio, craft. Use bounded layer roles, shared anchors, and named counterweights.

**Parity:** mobile recomposes into semantic sequence; static composition already carries hierarchy. Reject source-rights ambiguity, absolute-position chaos, or every layer competing.

## Index preview

**Fit:** scan-heavy portfolio, collection, article, or product index. Keep the list authoritative and enhance focused/selected rows with one preview.

**Parity:** touch selects explicitly or uses inline thumbnails; keyboard focus never becomes pointer-only preview. Reject obscured labels, repeated heavy media, or preview-driven layout shift.

## Mobile snap deck

**Fit:** a short comparison set or chapters. Use stable widths, scroll snapping only as enhancement, visible continuation, and direct controls.

**Parity:** logical DOM order and reduced-motion depth removal; desktop may become a grid or rail. Reject long copy, hidden actions, or too many items.

## Adaptive navigation

**Fit:** long narrative needing orientation and quiet conversion support. Reserve layout space and define expanded, compact, open, focus, and scrolled states.

**Parity:** skip link, conventional labels, safe-area-aware mobile bar, no gesture-only menu. Reject morphing that obscures location or actions.

## Spatial focal object

**Fit:** material/spatial behavior is essential to understanding. Load an approved poster first, expose an explicit enter control, tier quality by device, and keep conventional content/navigation outside the scene.

**Parity:** alternate views, semantic description, keyboard/touch controls, static detail path. Reject spectacle as navigation, unbounded rendering, or a concept equally clear as a still.
